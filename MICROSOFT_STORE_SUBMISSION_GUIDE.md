# Microsoft Partner Center — Store Submission Kit for BetterNote Pro Studio

This document contains everything you need to publish **BetterNote Pro Studio** to the **Microsoft Store** via the [Microsoft Partner Center Dashboard](https://partner.microsoft.com/en-US/dashboard/apps-and-games/overview).

---

## 🚀 Quick Step-by-Step Submission Walkthrough

### Step 1: Create a New Product in Partner Center
1. Navigate to: [Partner Center — Apps and Games Overview](https://partner.microsoft.com/en-US/dashboard/apps-and-games/overview).
2. Click the **"+ New product"** button in the top right.
3. Select **"Desktop application"** (or **"MSIX or PWA app"** depending on your enrollment).
4. Enter the product name: **`BetterNote Pro Studio - Pen & PDF Notebook`** (or `BetterNote PC`).
5. Click **"Check availability"** and then **"Reserve product name"**.
