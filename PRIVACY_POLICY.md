# Privacy Policy for BetterNote Pro Studio

**Effective Date:** September 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 15, 2026

BetterNote Pro Studio ("we", "our", or "the application") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, storage, and processing.

---

## 1. 100% Offline-First Architecture
BetterNote Pro Studio is designed as a standalone, offline-first desktop application for Windows 10 and Windows 11.
- **No Account Required**: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in to use any features of BetterNote Pro Studio.
- **No Cloud Tracking**: We do not run any remote tracking, analytics, telemetry, or behavioral surveillance servers.
- **Local Execution**: All handwriting rendering, QuickShape vector calculation, PDF parsing, and document editing occur strictly on your local PC hardware.

---

## 2. Personal Data & Note Content Storage
- **Local Storage**: All notebooks, drawings, handwritten strokes, inserted images, audio annotations, and settings are saved locally on your computer via Windows IndexedDB and local file storage.
- **Zero Third-Party Data Sharing**: Your notes, documents, and personal files are never transmitted to us or any third-party servers.
- **Optional Local Backup**: When you choose to configure a local backup folder or point to a local synchronization directory (such as a local Google Drive or OneDrive folder), files are written directly through your local operating system file API. No intermediary servers are involved.

---

## 3. Hardware & Sensor Permissions
- **Stylus & Touch (Pointer Events)**: BetterNote Pro Studio uses standard Windows Pointer Events (pen pressure, tilt, coordinate points) solely for real-time rendering of ink on your screen.
- **Clipboard Access**: When you paste an image (via Ctrl+V or the Paste button) or capture a snip, clipboard data is accessed solely within your active session to insert the image into your selected notebook page. No clipboard data is transmitted externally.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy
BetterNote Pro Studio does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect product updates or Microsoft Store certification standards. Any updates will be posted directly to our public GitHub repository.
